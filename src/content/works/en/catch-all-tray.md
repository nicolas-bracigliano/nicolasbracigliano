Five iterations to get the slot for the espresso tamper right. The magnets snap modules together so the layout follows what's actually on the desk this week — pens, calipers, a tray for whatever doesn't yet have a home.

PETG instead of PLA because the magnets push on the corner walls when modules click. PLA cracked in two prints. PETG holds.
