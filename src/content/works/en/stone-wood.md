The TV stand is the wrong piece of furniture for a small person who climbs. The fix isn't a different stand; it's deciding the screen is a feature of the wall, not a thing sitting on a table. A walnut shell, anchored to the studs, with the 75″ panel framed inside it instead of perched on top.

Walnut for the shell because rock and wood shout nature. A forest, a mountain. The pairing first landed when I visited Bariloche for the first time. The stone shelf cuts a horizontal line across the warm walnut; the electric fireplace on top of it earns the stone with a strip of light and motion.

Three iterations on paper, three quotes out, stone still undecided.
