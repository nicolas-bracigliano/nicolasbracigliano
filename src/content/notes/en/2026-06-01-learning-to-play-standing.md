I'm learning to play standing up. No gig to play, no band waiting. I just want to be able to.

The strap sits low, and the weight of the Les Paul lands on my shoulder in a way it never does in a chair. The fretting hand has to relearn its reach: the guitar hangs lower, the wrist comes in at a different angle, and the stretches that were automatic sitting down aren't anymore.

What I didn't expect is how much it changes what I want to play. Sitting, I work a riff slow and careful, the same bars over and over until they stick. Standing, with the strap low and the weight pulling down, the same guitar wants to be loud. The honest result is that I mostly just want to play punk rock, very loud.
