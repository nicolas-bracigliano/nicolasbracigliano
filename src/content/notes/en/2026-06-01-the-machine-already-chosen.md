The Barista Pro is a single boiler with the grinder built in, and I've run out of room on it. I want to push the recipe further and it won't let me: not enough control on the brew, a grinder I can't really dial, a steam wand that leaves the milk short of silky. And the single boiler means waiting between the shot and the jug.

So the plan was simple. A dedicated espresso machine, because most all-in-ones are mediocre. The Fellow Espresso (Series 1) is the one I keep coming back to.

Then the Meraki Gen 2 turned up. Grind and brew by volume, a rotary pump. That spec sheet made me look at an all-in-one with different eyes. It doesn't disprove the rule; most all-in-ones really are mediocre. The Meraki is just the exception. And the exception is enough that I don't know anymore whether I'm buying a brewer and a grinder, or abandoning the principle and getting an all-in-one.
